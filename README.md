# Public-Sector-Operations-Reporting-Platform
This project delivers a centralized reporting and analytics platform for monitoring the operational performance of Public Sector pension administration activities.


The solution consolidates data from multiple business systems, APIs, SharePoint repositories, forms, and Excel files into a unified reporting model that serves as the department's single source of truth for operational reporting and decision-making.

The platform automates data collection, transformation, validation, and reporting processes, enabling management and operational teams to monitor performance across key business functions through interactive Power BI dashboards.

## Business Challenge

Operational data was distributed across multiple systems and manually maintained reports, resulting in:

* Inconsistent reporting outputs
* Significant manual effort
* Delayed access to performance information
* Difficulty tracking departmental KPIs
* Limited visibility across business functions

The organization required a centralized reporting platform capable of providing timely, accurate, and actionable operational insights.

---

## Solution Overview

The solution extracts and consolidates operational data from multiple sources including:

* Core Business System APIs
* Operational Forms
* SharePoint Lists and Files
* Excel-Based Reports

Data is processed through automated transformation pipelines, standardized into a reporting model, and delivered through Power BI dashboards.

---

## Reporting Areas

The dashboard provides dedicated reporting pages for:

### Suspense Management

Monitoring unresolved transactions and exception cases.

### Benefit Processing

Tracking benefit applications, approvals, and payment activities.

### Allocations

Monitoring contribution allocation activities and processing performance.

### New Business

Tracking onboarding and registration of new members.

### Sensitization Activities

Reporting on outreach and awareness programs.

### Regulatory Reporting

Monitoring compliance and regulatory reporting requirements.

### Stakeholder Engagements

Tracking customer and institutional engagement activities.

### Retail Operations

Monitoring retail service performance and operational activities.

---

## Data Model

The reporting solution utilizes a centralized analytical model consisting of:

* Fact Tables
* Dimension Tables
* Enterprise Date Table
* Business KPI Measures

Key components include:

* 50+ DAX Measures
* Time Intelligence Calculations
* Performance Metrics
* Trend Analysis Indicators
* Operational KPIs
